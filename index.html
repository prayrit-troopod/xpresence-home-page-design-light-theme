Questions

How long does it last?

All three last 8+ hours on skin, with moderate projection: noticed within arm's length, never overpowering.

Can I try before buying a full bottle?

Yes. The Discovery Set (₹999) holds three 10 ml bottles of Focus, Warmth and Power. Wear each for a day, then choose your 30 ml.

Which one is right for me?

Focus is likeability that invites collaboration: pink pepper, cedarwood and sandalwood. Warmth is trust that builds loyalty: vanilla and florals. Power is credibility that inspires decision-making: saffron, Turkish rose and amber. Not sure? Take the 30-second quiz.

Is it unisex?

Yes. All three are 30 ml unisex Eau de Parfum.

What's in it?

More than half of every formula is natural ingredients. Every fragrance is IFRA compliant, cruelty-free, vegan and made in India.
