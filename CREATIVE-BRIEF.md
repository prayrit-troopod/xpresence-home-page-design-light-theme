# XPresence creative brief (from the client questionnaire)

The current images are generated placeholders. The client said they are "not quite right" overall: Focus only partly works, and Power and Warmth "should feel very different". **Every model shot must use Indian models.** The client also chose no "most on-brand" scene from the current set.

## Direction from the questionnaire

- **Feel:** confident, quiet luxury, bold, artisanal. Never cheap, loud, old-fashioned, overly feminine or masculine, or generic.
- **Admired brands:** Byredo, Aesop, Diptyque, Tom Ford.
- **Colour:** neutral base, with each product carrying its own colour strictly: Focus teal `#12514A`, Power navy `#1C2439`, Warmth terracotta `#D2614F`.
- **Audience:** 25 to 34, mostly women, metro cities. Corporate professionals, founders, frequent travellers.
- **Planned content:** product photography, product video, a campaign shoot, UGC and influencer content.
