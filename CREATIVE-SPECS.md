# XPresence home page: creative specs

Measured on the live layout on 2026-10-19 at desktop (1440 wide) and phone (375 wide). Display sizes are CSS pixels. **Export size** is the file to generate: at least 2× the largest display size, in the aspect ratio that survives both the desktop and phone crops. Every image is cropped with `object-fit: cover`, so keep the subject inside the **safe area** noted.

General: sRGB, WebP (or high-quality JPG/PNG and I convert), photos ≤ 400 KB after export, videos H.264 MP4, muted, 8–15 s seamless loop, ≤ 8 MB. Indian models only (client brief).
