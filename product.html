Questions, answered

How long does it last?

8+ hours on skin. It lasts a little longer on moisturised skin and on clothes, and a little less on dry skin or in strong heat.

Is it too strong for the office?

No. Projection is moderate: noticed within arm's length, never overpowering. Two or three sprays are enough for a full working day.

Does it work in Indian heat?

Why does it smell different on different people?

Skin chemistry. Your skin's oils, warmth and pH change how the notes open and settle. That is why we suggest wearing it for a full day before you decide.

Can I try it before buying 30 ml?

Yes. The Discovery Set (₹999) holds three 10 ml bottles of Focus, Warmth and Power, plus ₹300 off your first 30 ml.

Is it unisex?

Yes. All three are 30 ml unisex Eau de Parfum, made to be worn by anyone.

How should I store it?

Capped, in its box, somewhere cool and dark: a drawer, not the bathroom or a sunny windowsill. Stored well, it keeps its character for years.
